## The problem

The constraint they were actually working against, in their terms. Volume,
budget, scale, an object that won't hold still, an audience that has to be
able to repeat the result. Two or three sentences. Resist the urge to make it
about us yet.

## What they did

The solution, described concretely enough to reproduce: rig, lighting,
background, distance, lens, post-processing, sequencing, how the images are
captioned and numbered. Where you're inferring rather than quoting, say so.

## What we take

The transferable part, and the part that doesn't transfer. Tie it to a
specific rule in [[Photographing the exhibition|notes.photos]] — legibility,
declared scale, repeatable capture, visible attribution — or explain why it
argues against one.

broke. Delete the heading if we haven't.
